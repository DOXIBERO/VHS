# PART ONE: THE ARCHITECTURE OF ASCENSION

## Total Step Count: 30 Steps to the Summit

The staircase is divided into **6 ZONES**, each containing **5 STEPS**. Every zone has a distinct cosmic visual theme that evolves as players climb higher into the universe, and each zone covers a pillar of the A0 curriculum.

| ZONE | STEPS | CURRICULUM PILLAR | COSMIC BIOME |
|------|-------|-------------------|--------------|
| **ZONE 1: DER ATEM** *(The Breath)* | 1–5 | German Alphabet & Core Sounds | Raw concrete dissolving into asteroid field, grey-brown rocks floating in starlight |
| **ZONE 2: DER KLANG** *(The Sound)* | 6–10 | Special Sounds (Ä, Ö, Ü, CH, SCH, ß, EI, EU, IE) & Diphthongs | Crystalline nebula, platforms are translucent quartz resonating with sound waves |
| **ZONE 3: DIE ZAHL** *(The Number)* | 11–15 | Numbers 0–20 + basic counting constructs | Binary star system, platforms orbit twin suns, gravity shifts between them |

| **ZONE 6: DAS VERB** *(The Verb)* | 26–30 | sein, haben, gehen, kommen, machen, sprechen + present tense conjugation + first micro-sentences | Supermassive black hole horizon — platforms float along the accretion disk, light bending around the edge of understanding |

# PART TWO: THE LOBBY — ZN9A (الزنقة)

## 2.1 Environment Design

The lobby is a **hyper-detailed, photorealistic Moroccan urban alleyway** rendered with warm evening light. Not a tourist postcard — this is *authentic street*:

- **Cracked ochre walls** with sun-bleached movie posters and faded spray paint
- A **plastic chair** knocked over next to a small metal table with mint tea glasses
- **Electrical wires** crisscrossing overhead between buildings
- A **stray cat** sleeping on a satellite dish
- Faint sounds: a distant call to prayer ending, a moped engine, Arabic hip-hop from a phone speaker somewhere
- The **industrial metal garage door** (rideau de fer) at the dead end of the alley, rust-orange, padlocked, covered in sticker residue — it looks like it hasn't opened in years

## 2.2 Player Spawn & Bean Assignment

Players load in one at a time. Each spawn triggers:

1. A **lottery machine animation** in the corner of the HUD — a glass sphere full of colored balls tumbles, one ball shoots out
2. The ball POPS and the player's bean materializes in that color on the street
3. Colors are **intentionally absurd and specific**: not just "red" or "blue" — colors like *Rotten Pistachio*, *Sunburned Flamingo*, *Grandma's Couch Beige*, *Existential Crisis Purple*, *Tax Return Grey*
4. The color name appears in **both German and English** above the bean — this is the player's first passive German exposure: "FARBE: Flamingo-Rosa"

## 2.3 Bean Idle Behavior in Lobby

While waiting for others to READY up:

- Beans waddle around the alley with **exaggerated physics** — they bounce off walls, trip on the plastic chair, startle the cat
- Players can **tap the mint tea glasses** to make them clink (a shared musical toy)
- The webcam feed is already live: players see each other's **giant eyes and mouths** on the beans, so the lobby is already hilarious before the game even begins
- Players are blinking, laughing, eating snacks in their chairs — all magnified on the marshmallow bodies

## 2.4 The READY System

- Each player gets a single large **"BEREIT" (READY)** button
- When pressed, their bean sits down cross-legged facing the garage door
- A small German counter appears: *"3 von 4 Spieler bereit"* — another passive exposure
- When all are seated: **every UI element — the button, the HUD, the color name — smoothly animates downward and dissolves into the street like water soaking into concrete**

*The screen is now purely cinematic. No interface. Just the alley, the beans, and the garage.*

# PART THREE: THE DARK MATTER TRANSITION

## BEAT 1: THE WALK (8 seconds)
- The beans stand up in unison and begin walking toward the garage door in a loose cluster.
- Steadicam tracking shot from behind, slightly low angle.
- Footsteps on concrete, distant alley sounds fade, 20Hz sub-bass drone begins.

## BEAT 2: THE DOOR (6 seconds)
- Beans stop in front of the door. Padlock cracks and falls.
- Rideau de fer rattles upward with industrial screech.
- Beyond the door: absolute void. Beans walk inside.

## BEAT 3: THE SEAL (4 seconds)
- Camera follows in, rotates 180° to face the door from within.
- Garage door slams down with concussive boom. Total blackout.

## BEAT 4: THE SILENCE (1.5 seconds)
- Pure black screen, dead digital silence. Palette cleanser.

## BEAT 5: THE LAMP (Player-Triggered)
- Faint warm filament glow in upper screen. Prompt: "Klick."
- On click: old industrial electric lamp surges to life with electric thunk.
- Warm light swings on cable, casting swaying shadows across raw concrete walls and floor.
- Beans look around with giant webcam eyes blinking.

## BEAT 6: THE GLITCH (3 seconds)
- Digital glitch on concrete. Walls stutter in two positions simultaneously.
- Reversed whisper: "Willkommen".
- Walls and ceiling dissolve into floating upward light particles.

## BEAT 7: THE REVEAL (5 seconds)
- Infinite cosmic vista: stars, nebulae, galaxy arm.
- Garage floor transforms into luminous dark stone: STEP 1 of a colossal staircase.
- Crane camera pulls back and up showing 30 steps ascending into stars.
- Score enters: crystalline choir chord.
- Title in sky: DRJA — DIE TREPPE DER SPRACHE (30 Stufen bis A1).

# PART FOUR: CHARACTER DESIGN — THE COMEDY ENGINE

- Body: Marshmallow-imposter hybrid, pear-shaped center of gravity, wobbles and deforms on impact (squash-and-stretch).
- Face: Real-time webcam feed mapping enlarged eyes (2.8x) and mouth (3.2x) with cartoon saturation, 1px outline, and subtle fisheye distortion.
- German mouth shapes (Ü, Ö, CH, R, SCH) look comically exaggerated on bouncing beans.
- Emotes: Up = "JA!", Down = "NEIN!", Left = "HILFE!", Right = "TANZ!".
