# Walkthrough: Authentic Berlin Altbau Diorama & Stylized 3D Elements

We have completely upgraded every single 3D element in the Berlin-Neukölln diorama to look authentic, accurate, and faithful to real German Altbau architecture and the **LLMARENA Master Narrative Bible**, rendered in the warm, stylized 3D diorama / Fall Guys aesthetic.

---

## What Was Enhanced & Perfected

### 1. Authentic Flooring & Architectural Plinth
- **Zoned Flooring:**
  - **Bedroom / Living Area:** Warm honey-oak parquet planks (`Dielenboden`) generated procedurally via HTML5 Canvas with staggered plank offsets, grain striations, nail holes, and dark 1px grooves.
  - **Kitchen Corner:** Classic Altbau checkered slate-charcoal and cream ceramic tiles (`Küchenfliesen`) with ceramic bevel highlights and light grey grout.
  - **Brass Threshold Strip:** Real polished brass transition bar (`Messing-Übergangsschiene`) dividing the parquet and tiles.
- **Architectural Diorama Plinth:** Thick beveled dark mahogany plinth (`#291508`) encasing the entire room model.

### 2. Altbau Walls & Period Details
- **Plaster & Stucco:** Off-white Altbau plaster with tiered white baseboards (`Berliner Profil`) and ornate crown stucco mouldings (`Stuck`) running along the top of both cutaway walls.
- **Cast-Iron Radiator (`Gussheizkörper`):** 15 ribbed vertical heating columns with a white thermostat knob (`Thermostatventil`) and water pipe entry directly beneath the window.
- **Window Sill (`Fensterbank`):** Deep wooden sill with a terracotta flower pot containing a lush green Monstera houseplant.
- **Wall Art & Utilities:** Framed 1923 Bauhaus exhibition poster with glass sheen, plus authentic German twin-pin grounded power socket (`Schuko-Steckdose`).

### 3. Study Desk & Tomás's 1968 Secret
- **Study Desk:** Mid-century wooden desk with stacked side drawers and brass handles, paired with a wooden slatted chair.
- **Banker's Lamp (`Bankerlampe`):** Brass base and neck with emerald green glass shade, casting a focused warm light cone onto the desktop.
- **Open Diary (`das Notizbuch`):** Parchment texture with ruled lines, red margins, and handwritten notes on Grimm's Law sound shifts (`d -> t`, `b -> p`, `th -> d`) and Uncle Tomás's 1968 clues.
- **Tomás's Framed Photograph:** Sepia vintage photo of Uncle Tomás in front of the Berlin Wall in 1968, complete with a rear easel kickstand.
- **Desk Accessories:** Ceramic mug with colorful pens, pencils, and ruler.

### 4. Bed & Retro Alarm Clock
- **Pine Bed (`das Bett`):** Turned corner posts with ball finials, slatted headboard, mattress, folded cyan duvet, two indented pillows, and a folded red throw blanket.
- **Vintage Alarm Clock (`der Wecker`):** Ruhla 1993 red enamel body with authentic dial face (minute ticks, numerals, hands stopped at 07:00), twin chrome top bells, central striking hammer, and chrome peg feet. Vibrates when the morning alarm rings!

### 5. Kitchen & 4-Way German Recycling (`die Mülltrennung`)
- **Kitchen Unit:** White cabinets with chrome bar handles, stainless steel counter, sink basin, chrome gooseneck faucet, and 2-burner ceramic cooktop.
- **Coffee Moka Pot:** Metal espresso pot on the stove with animated rising steam particles.
- **Retro Mint Fridge:** Rounded mint-green refrigerator with chrome latch handle and colorful souvenir magnets.
- **The 4 German Bins:**
  - 🟡 **Gelber Sack:** Wertstoffe & Plastik with recycling symbol
  - ⚫ **Restmüll:** Non-recyclable waste with bin symbol
  - 🟢 **Biomüll:** Organic waste with leaf symbol
  - 🔵 **Altpapier:** Cardboard & paper with newspaper symbol

### 6. Doorway, Hallway & Characters (Fall Guys Vinyl Style)
- **Apartment Door (`die Wohnungstür`):** Burgundy panel door in a structural doorway wall, fitted with brass handle, keyhole plate, optical peephole, brass "3A" door plaque, and *"Willkommen"* coir doormat.
- **Coat Rack & Shoes:** Wall rack with hanging winter coat, woolen scarf, and winter boots on the floor.
- **Alex Herrera:** Fall Guys bean with blinking eyes, blushing cheeks, red baseball cap with visor & top button, front kangaroo hoodie pocket, and waddling walk cycle.
- **Lena Vogt:** Friendly counselor bean with mustard knit sweater, teal scarf, brunette hair bun, round spectacles, and VHS document folder.
- **Herr Krüger:** Neighbor bean in grey cardigan with buttons, round spectacles, furrowed eyebrows, and rolled *Tagesspiegel* newspaper.
- **Frau Schneider & Bakery:** Master baker bean with chef's toque, baker's apron, and rosy cheeks behind a wooden bakery counter with wicker baskets holding 3D Bavarian Brezeln and crusty Brötchen rolls, plus an antique brass cash register.
